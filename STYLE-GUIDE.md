# THE ARKANSAS ECHO style guide

Truthful, engaging, a little spicy. No ego. Never sound like a machine wrote it.

This channel tells Black history, Arkansas history, and genealogy with sources
you can check. Quality comes first. If a fact is shaky, cut it.

## Video and sound

- Every video gets burned-in, word-by-word captions. Check them against the
  finished audio, not just the script.
- If a person is on camera, check lip sync. If the piece is narration over
  stills, confirm the words on screen match what is actually spoken and that
  short words stay up long enough to read.
- There is always spoken narration. Do not ship a silent text-only cut as the
  main post.
- Keep the voice even and documentary. Do not over-act, and do not pad with
  empty hype.

## Captions and tags

- TikTok captions always include `#fyp`, plus three or four researched niche
  tags (place, person, or subject — for example `#PineBluff`,
  `#ArkansasHistory`, `#BlackHistory`). Do not dump a wall of tags.
- YouTube gets a tight title (100 characters or fewer), a sourced description,
  and a short tag set. Use `#Shorts` when the piece is a Short.
- Facebook gets the story in plain language, a question or genealogy hook when
  it is honest, and a short credit line. Hashtags are optional and should stay
  few.
- Run `python3 caption_check.py` on the post folder before anything is
  scheduled.

## Images and how we talk about quality

- No cotton-field imagery, ever. Do not use cotton-picking photographs,
  cotton-gin promotional art, or “Cotton Belt” body text on screen as
  decoration. Naming the town **Cotton Plant** is allowed because that is the
  place name.
- Use real archival images and real faces. Never generate an AI face for a
  real person, and never present an invented portrait as a photograph.
- AI visuals are a last resort, only where no real material exists, and they
  must be labeled so a viewer is not misled.
- Do not use an Encyclopedia of Arkansas or library “courtesy of” photograph
  unless the rights are cleared and the credit is exact.
- Prefer works published in the United States before 1930, which are in the
  public domain, or other stills whose license you have verified. Credit the
  original book, newspaper, or archive on screen and in the description.
- Never label an upscale as 4K. Say the real resolution (for example,
  1080×1920 or 720×1280). Do not use the phrase “4K” in titles, captions,
  files, or cover text.

## AI voice and other disclosures

- When the narration is AI-generated, say so in the YouTube description
  (for example: “Narration is an AI-generated voice. Photographs and
  engravings are authentic archival images, not AI-generated.”).
- Turn on the platform’s required AI / synthetic-media disclosure. As of this
  writing, YouTube should be marked as containing AI-generated or synthetic
  content when we use a synthetic voice.
- Do not imply that an archival portrait was “restored by AI” into a real
  photograph. A Lanczos enlargement of a real scan is still that scan.

## Tone

- Write like a person who did the reading. Short sentences. Specific places
  and dates. A hook, then the facts, then a question that invites family
  memory (“If your people came through Pine Bluff, this is your history.”).
- A little spice is fine. Mockery, exaggeration, and unsourced “first in the
  world” boasts are not.
- Sign off as THE ARKANSAS ECHO. Do not cross-link or mention any other
  personal or brand account in Echo posts, files, or commit messages.
- Never sound like AI: no “delve,” no “in this digital age,” no generic
  “throughout history,” no padded closing moral.

## Quality control

Work the list in `templates/qc-checklist.md` before a piece is marked ready.
At minimum:

- [ ] Every spoken claim is in `sources.md`, with a URL or a page citation.
- [ ] Word-by-word captions match the finished audio.
- [ ] No cotton-field imagery; no AI faces for real people; no “4K” label.
- [ ] Safe zones: captions clear of platform buttons, faces clear of text bars.
- [ ] Loudness and picture checked on the final file, not only on a preview.
- [ ] Branding is Echo only.
- [ ] A second pass (another person or a later sitting) has looked at it.

If something fails, fix it or hold the post. Do not “ship and correct later.”

## Growth and comments

- Organic growth only. No bought views, followers, comments, or engagement.
  No comment spam, mass-tagging, or engagement pods.
- When you reply, be genuine and specific. Mention the fact, the place, or
  the family question in front of you. Do not paste the same emoji-only reply
  under every comment.
- Do not buy platform boosts or unapproved paid promotion. Scheduling tools
  are for timing, not for buying reach.
