# Wiley Jones — narration script

Spoken on the posted short. Punctuation is for reading and the record;
word-level timings from the finished encode are not stored in this repo
(large media stays hosted, not committed).

Born enslaved. Died the richest Black man in Arkansas. And the streetcars
in Pine Bluff? Those were his.

Walter Wiley Jones was born in Georgia in 1848 and brought to Jefferson
County as a child. His father, a white planter, said he'd left papers to
free the family. The papers were never found. In 1858, they were sold.

After emancipation, Jones cut hair in a Pine Bluff barbershop. He waited
tables for his meals. He saved every dollar and loaned money at interest.
Then he went into business for himself: real estate, a mercantile, a
fifty-five-acre park with a racetrack. In August 1886 he secured a charter
for a mule-drawn streetcar line. The first car rolled on October 19th. By
1893, a Pine Bluff city book said his consolidated lines ran about twelve
miles and put his fortune at two hundred thousand dollars. He never
married. He died on December 7th, 1904.

If your people came through Pine Bluff, this is your history. So tell me:
did your Arkansas history class ever mention Wiley Jones?

This is The Arkansas Echo.
