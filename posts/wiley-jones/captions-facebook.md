Born enslaved. Died the richest Black man in Arkansas. And the streetcars in Pine Bluff? Those were his.

Walter Wiley Jones, born in Georgia in 1848, built a fifty-five-acre park with a racetrack and owned Pine Bluff’s mule-drawn streetcars. A city book of 1893 put his fortune at $200,000. He died December 7, 1904.

If your people came through Pine Bluff, this is your history. Did your Arkansas history class ever mention Wiley Jones?
