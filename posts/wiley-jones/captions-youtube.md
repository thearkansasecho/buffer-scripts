**Title:** Born Enslaved. Died the Richest Black Man in Arkansas.

**Description:**

Born enslaved. Died the richest Black man in Arkansas. And the streetcars in Pine Bluff? Those were his.

Walter Wiley Jones was born in Georgia in 1848 and brought to Jefferson County as a child. The freedom papers his father said he had left were never found. The family was sold in 1858. After emancipation he cut hair, waited tables, saved, and loaned money at interest, then built a fifty-five-acre park with a racetrack and a mule-drawn streetcar line. A Pine Bluff city book of 1893 put his consolidated lines at about twelve miles and his fortune at $200,000. He died on December 7, 1904.

If your people came through Pine Bluff, this is your history. Did your Arkansas history class ever mention Wiley Jones?
