## Designed cards (not photographs)

On-screen fact and quote panels are original Echo designs. Where they quote the court, the wording is taken from the Supreme Court of Arkansas joint press release of November 5, 2026. The credit under those cards reads: “Wording from the Supreme Court of Arkansas joint press release, Nov. 5, 2026. Designed card; exact official wording, not a facsimile of the page.” Official PDF scans were not used as visible backgrounds in the finished video, because the small type competed with captions and could be misread as a facsimile.

- Two April 2024 Xiquinho Silva photographs in which the Confederate Women of Arkansas monument is front and center: moved to `images/not-used/` and never framed.
- CC BY-SA Capitol photographs (other photographers): held back so the finished video is not forced into a share-alike license.

- Office of Professional Conduct Order of Interim Suspension, CPC-2026-036 (Nov. 5, 2026): a real disciplinary order, held for research; its findings are not a criminal conviction and are not shown on screen.
- Official joint-press-release and Baker press-release page scans: transcribed and quoted; not used as Ken Burns backgrounds in the finished file.
