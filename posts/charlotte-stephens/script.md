# Charlotte Andrews Stephens — narration script

Approved spoken script from the rebuilt short. Status: posted November 12, 2026, America/Chicago.

She was fifteen. Her teacher got sick. Charlotte Andrews Stephens stepped
up—and made Little Rock history. In 1869, she became the city’s first
Black public-school teacher. Her career spanned seventy years. She used
her teaching money to study at Oberlin, and Little Rock named Stephens
Elementary for her in 1910. She retired in 1939. A teenager answered the
call. Generations felt the impact. Remember her name: Charlotte Andrews
Stephens. This is The Arkansas Echo.
