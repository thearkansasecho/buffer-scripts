# Charlotte Andrews Stephens — sources and licenses

## Reference sources (opened and corroborated)

- CALS Encyclopedia of Arkansas, “Charlotte Andrews Stephens.”
  Confirms age fifteen, the ill teacher, 1869, first Black public-school
  teacher in Little Rock (not first in Arkansas), a seventy-year career,
  teaching savings used to study at Oberlin, the 1910 school naming, and
  1939 retirement.
  https://encyclopediaofarkansas.net/entries/charlotte-andrews-1772/
- John Kirk, UA Little Rock / KUAR, “Charlotte Andrews Stephens,”
  January 30, 2016.
  https://www.ualrpublicradio.org/2016-01-30/charlotte-andrews-stephens

## Facts to keep precise

- First Black public-school teacher in **Little Rock**, not first in
  Arkansas.
- Career spanned seventy years; educational leaves mean do not describe
  uninterrupted classroom teaching.
- She studied at Oberlin; do not claim an Oberlin degree unless a source
  for the degree is in hand.
