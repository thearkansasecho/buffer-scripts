At fifteen, Charlotte Andrews Stephens stepped in for her sick teacher. In 1869, she became Little Rock’s first Black public-school teacher. Her career spanned seventy years; she retired in 1939. Little Rock named Stephens Elementary for her in 1910.

Did your family attend Stephens Elementary? This is Arkansas history worth knowing.
